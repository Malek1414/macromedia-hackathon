What if AI ran the planet? 2026 to 2076

## Description

What if an AI got the same budget as all of us and ran the planet for 50 years? AI Runs the Planet plays 5,000 possible futures from 2026 to 2076 on a 3D globe: the best case, the most likely and the scariest. Then you take the sliders and try to beat the AI.
Code, results and how the model works: https://github.com/Malek1414/macromedia-hackathon
Possible futures, not predictions. Made by Malek Hassan and Sammy for the Macromedia X CODE Hackathon, Berlin, Nov 2026.
