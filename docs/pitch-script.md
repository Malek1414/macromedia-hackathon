# Voice-over script: AI Runs the Planet (2:00)

Read one beat a time, with a 1 s pause between beats. The on-screen action for each timecode is in [video-spec.md](video-spec.md). This passed the pitch gates: every figure is in the fact ledger `pitch/ai-runs-the-planet/facts.json`, and three test judges retold all four key points. Don't change a number without changing the ledger.

## Story key (what every judge must be able to say back)
- **who:** everyone who will still be alive in 2076, starting with the young people in this room
- **broken:** the debate about letting AI run the world is all opinions and headlines, and nobody can see the odds of where each choice leads
- **changed:** AI Runs the Planet gives an AI and the audience the same 360-point budget, plays thousands of futures to 2076 and shows the best, most likely and scariest one on a globe
- **proof:** the AI kept 92% of futures below 2 degrees but put 1 point into coastal protection, so 31 million people are pushed out by the sea in its most likely future
